How StyleAI Works

Combining MediaPipe 468 3D facial landmark tracking, geometric ratio calculations, and Google Gemini AI generative styling advice.

1. 468 3D Facial Landmarks

Our pipeline utilizes MediaPipe Face Mesh to map 468 precise 3D surface coordinates on the face in real-time, detecting subtle facial contours regardless of lighting or angle.

2. Geometric Ratio Analysis

Computes mathematical proportions: Width-to-Length ratio, Forehead-to-Jawline ratio, Cheekbone prominence index, and Chin roundness factor to classify one of 7 canonical face shapes.

3. Generative AI Advice

Leverages Google Gemini API to synthesize custom styling advice, explaining why specific layered, bob, fade, or fringe styles complement your face structure.

4. Privacy-First Pipeline

Photos are processed securely in memory and never shared with third parties. Analysis records remain strictly within your personal user account.
